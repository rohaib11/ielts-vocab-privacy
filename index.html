Privacy Policy for IELTS Vocab Pro

Last updated: September 2026

Introduction

IELTS Vocab Pro ("the App", "we", "us") is developed by Rohaib Tech. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Summary

IELTS Vocab Pro does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The App works entirely offline and stores all data locally on your device.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, request, or have access to:

  • Your name, email address, or any contact information
  • Your location
  • Your device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Your usage patterns, analytics, or behavioral data
  • Any content you create within the App

Information Stored on Your Device

IELTS Vocab Pro stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your learning progress (words reviewed, quiz scores, streaks)
  • Your preferences (accent choice, reminder time)
  • Your favorites and personal settings

This data never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to us or any third party.

Permissions Used

IELTS Vocab Pro requests the following Android permissions:

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used only when you choose to record your pronunciation for self-comparison. Recordings are stored temporarily on your device and are deleted when you leave the pronunciation screen.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to send optional daily study reminders that you can enable or disable at any time in the App settings.
  • Storage access (via File Picker): Used only when you choose to import or export a backup file that you select yourself.

Third-Party Services

IELTS Vocab Pro does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash reporting services.

Children's Privacy

IELTS Vocab Pro does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The App is safe for use by all ages.

Data Backup

You may export a backup of your local data at any time from within the App. This backup file is created and stored entirely by you. We have no access to it.

Data Deletion

You can delete all data stored by the App at any time by uninstalling the App, or by using the "Reset Progress" option in the App settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted at this URL. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
